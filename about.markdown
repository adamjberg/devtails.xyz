## Hello, my name is Adam

I am a **developer**

> A developer is a person or entity that builds, creates, or expands something into a more advanced or usable state

Who writes **tales**

> A tale is a narrative that relates the details of an event, an adventure, or a series of events—either true or fictitious.

## Why dev/tails?

I want to share what I build ([dev](/dev)), but I still wanted the freedom to write a tale ([tails](/tails)) about pretty much anything.

When I was a kid, I loved playing Sonic the Hedgehog 2 on the Sega Genesis. 

As the younger sibling, I was destined to be player two (aka [Tails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tails_(Sonic_the_Hedgehog))). 

In two player mode, the game still really only cares about Sonic. The camera follows Sonic and sometimes I would find myself completely offscreen only to be teleported back to where Sonic actually was.  

Some might not have considered this position fun. However, it came with a significant benefit: Tails could infinitely respawn. 

**Lessons learned:** 
- When losing a life isn't on the line, you have complete freedom to experiment, take risks, and keep flying back into the frame
- You don't need the camera directly on you to make an impact 
- Play the hand you're dealt, take hits for the team, and never stop getting back up
